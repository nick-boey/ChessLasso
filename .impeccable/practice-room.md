# Practice room

Mode: Operate. The learner thinks, types a move, and discusses it with the coach. Board and chat remain the primary surface on desktop and mobile. History is illustrative; coaching is explicitly scripted.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal study room, structured like a tournament scoresheet opened alongside a conversation. One large board and a move-scoped coach feed; no metric dashboard in the playing surface.

OWN-WORLD: Blue-grey surroundings, white work surfaces, deep petrol ink, muted wooden board and restrained green accents. Manrope is a legible, self-hosted workhorse face. Simple hairlines and precise spacing carry hierarchy.

STORY: The learner arrives at a position, considers a plan, types algebraic notation, and asks for help when useful. A worked example opens its own board without replacing the game.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim logo/navigation header, followed directly by a compact coach-set task heading/subtitle and board on the left, with a move-scoped coach feed on the right. No opponent metadata, manual New game or individual-game download controls. Move entry is a single input with play/notation icons directly beneath the board. Every accepted move reveals its new instruction section; earlier discussions stay attached to their positions. Mobile stacks board, entry and coach. Signature interaction: a coach message opens an annotated move sequence in a separate board.

FORM: Tournament scoresheet and study companion, candidate 6 of seven grounded forms (club desk, lesson notebook, correspondence letter, analysis workstation, match scorecard, tournament scoresheet, library reading room). Seed 20e39631 ran with no catalog challengers. User's agreed simple board-and-chat brief and instruction to proceed govern the layout; build directly in code.
